Background Research & Problem Definition

Household cabinets often contain medicines, cleaners, detergents, and sharp tools, yet many existing safety systems are either too inconvenient, too bulky, or too easy to ignore in everyday life. NexLock One Touch is built around a simple goal: make child safety more secure, more practical, and more consistent in real homes.

Problem Definition

Short Thesis Statement

Young children can gain access to dangerous household items in seconds, and many current cabinet locks fail because they are inconvenient for adults to use consistently. NexLock One Touch addresses this problem by designing a cabinet safety solution that is compact, reliable, and simple enough to become part of everyday family routines.

Narrative

Why This Problem Matters

For young children, curiosity is natural. Cabinets, drawers, and low storage spaces are especially attractive because they are easy to reach and often contain unfamiliar objects. In many homes, these spaces hold medicines, cleaning products, dishwasher pods, detergents, and sharp kitchen tools. What appears to be an ordinary cabinet can therefore become a serious safety risk in only a few seconds.

The challenge is not simply that dangerous items exist in the home. The deeper issue is that existing child-safety products often do not fit naturally into daily life. If a lock is bulky, visually disruptive, frustrating to open, or difficult to install, adults are less likely to use it consistently. That inconsistency creates vulnerability. A strong safety design must therefore do more than block access: it must also encourage reliable, everyday use.

Target Market

Target Market Demographics

Primary Users

Parents, guardians, and caregivers responsible for homes with toddlers and young children.

Child Age Range

Most relevant for children ages 1–5, when mobility, curiosity, and unsupervised exploration increase.

Home Environments

Kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and utility spaces where hazardous materials are commonly stored.

User Need

A lock that is quick for adults, difficult for children, visually clean, and realistic for everyday use.

Conclusion

What the Research Shows

The research makes one point clear: the danger is not hypothetical. Children can reach harmful items quickly, and many families do not consistently use existing safety tools because those tools do not fit smoothly into everyday life. This creates a strong need for a cabinet-locking system that combines security, convenience, and clean design. NexLock One Touch is intended to meet that need.
