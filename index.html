Abstract

NexLock One Touch is a cabinet safety solution created to prevent young children from accessing medicines, cleaning products, sharp tools, and other dangerous household items. Our project combines research, concept development, engineering design, and reflection to create a product that is secure, practical, and realistic for everyday home use.

Research Highlights

Our background research showed that many current child-safety products fail not because the idea of safety is weak, but because the products are inconvenient, bulky, or difficult to use consistently in real homes.

Unsafe cabinet access remains a real household risk.

Many existing locks are visually bulky or inconvenient to use.

We explored multiple directions before selecting a final approach.

Our chosen concept emphasizes secure and modern access control.

Selected Direction

Biometric Deadbolt System

Our final concept uses fingerprint-based access to create a more secure and modern alternative to traditional cabinet locks.

Why We Chose It

  • Strong security and controlled access
  • Modern, innovative design identity
  • High user appeal compared with common locks
