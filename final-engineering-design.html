Iterations

Images of Various Design Iterations

Iteration 1

Iteration 2

Early iterations focused on creating a miniature prototype to test whether the internal components could function together within a compact enclosure. During this phase, multiple filament materials and print settings were tested to evaluate durability, print quality, and structural reliability. The orange prototype shown represented one of the less successful material tests, helping identify weaknesses in both strength and fit before moving toward a refined design.

Iteration 3

Iteration 4

The second design phase focused on improving component spacing, internal organization, and hook geometry. Additional sample-scale prototypes were created to determine the ideal hook angle, latch positioning, and enclosure dimensions needed for smooth locking and unlocking. These iterations helped refine the mechanical movement of the system while ensuring the components could fit together more efficiently and reliably.

Iteration 5

Iteration 6

Iteration 7

The final iterations transitioned from experimental prototypes to a fully refined functional design with accurate sizing, an integrated handle, optimized hook and latch geometry, and improved overall structure. These versions focused on usability, stability, and manufacturability while maintaining a compact and visually clean appearance. Feedback and testing from earlier prototypes were incorporated to create a more polished and reliable final product ready for implementation.

These iterations show how the NexLock design evolved through testing, feedback, and engineering improvements.
