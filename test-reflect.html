Testing + Iteration

Test & Reflect

We tested NexLock One Touch with users to evaluate security, ease of use, attachment strength, and overall product experience.

Testing Scenarios

Users were asked to complete realistic tasks that tested the main functions of the prototype.

  • Scenario 1: Try to open the cabinet while it is locked.
  • Scenario 2: Use the fingerprint system to unlock the cabinet.
  • Scenario 3: Close the cabinet and observe whether it locks automatically.
  • Scenario 4: Leave the cabinet open and observe whether the buzzer activates.

Test Results

Overall, NexLock received strong feedback from users. Most users rated the product highly in ease of use, functionality, and concept, with a majority of responses giving scores of 4 or 5.

70% gave a perfect 5/5 rating
80% rated the product a 4 or 5
60% mentioned attachment concerns

Reviewer Feedback

Users praised the product for being simple, efficient, and visually acceptable for a kitchen or home environment.

“A single touch of a fingerprint unlocks it.”
“It is efficient.”
“It doesn’t make my kitchen look ugly.”

Main Weakness Found

The biggest issue was attachment stability. Multiple users said the product needed to stay attached more securely and should not loosen or fall off when unlocked for too long.

  • Some users said to “make sure it stays on.”
  • Others said it “needs to attach to the cabinets.”
  • Several users noticed it could become loose over time.

Reflections and Design Iterations

The feedback confirmed that the core concept is strong because users understood the system quickly and liked the fingerprint-based unlocking. However, the attachment concerns showed that the physical mounting system needed the most improvement.

  • What worked: The fingerprint unlocking system was easy to understand and use.
  • What needed improvement: The lock needed a stronger and more reliable attachment method.
  • How feedback shaped the design: Future iterations will focus on stronger mounting, smaller sizing, and more adaptable attachment options for different cabinets and handles.

Future Work / Improvements

  • Improve the attachment system so the product does not loosen or fall.
  • Use stronger and more durable materials.
  • Make the overall lock smaller and more visually appealing.
  • Create adjustable parts so the lock fits more cabinet and handle types.
  • Add a stronger locking system for better long-term reliability.
  • Lower production cost to make the product more affordable.

Marketing Plan

NexLock would be marketed toward parents and families who want a safer home without complicated or unattractive childproofing products.

  • Promote the product as simple, secure, and modern.
  • Highlight the one-touch fingerprint unlocking feature.
  • Use social media videos showing the product working on cabinets.
  • Sell through parenting websites, home safety stores, and online marketplaces.
  • Emphasize peace of mind, convenience, and child safety.

Modern security with a smooth, easy look.
